Privacy Policy

How Recipe Vault collects, displays, protects, and manages your information.

1. What Your App Saves and Why

  • Account Credentials: Saves your login details (Email & Password or GitHub OAuth) via Supabase Authentication so you can securely log into your personal vault session.
  • Profile Information: Stores your chosen username in the profiles database table so your display name appears alongside recipes you share.
  • Recipe Data & Comments: Saves the titles, ingredient lists, instructions, and comments you submit so they persist in the community feed.
  • Uploaded Attachments: Stores uploaded images and PDFs in Supabase Storage (recipe-media) to display inline photos or document previews on your posts.

2. Who Can See Your Information

  • Public Recipe Feed: Any user accessing the vault (on desktop or mobile) can view published recipes, attached media files, and author usernames.
  • Staff & Moderation Access: Authorized Admins and Moderators can view internal User IDs (UUIDs), roles, and account activity to maintain community safety, send direct staff messages, hide posts, or manage user access.
  • Private Passwords: No one—including app administrators or moderators—can view your password. Credentials are encrypted and handled exclusively by Supabase Auth.

3. How to Delete or Manage Your Information

  • Self-Service Recipe Deletion: You can permanently delete any recipe you uploaded (along with its attached file) at any time using the Delete button on your post.
  • Moderation & Account Removal: Admins and Moderators can hide posts, delete uploaded files, or permanently delete user accounts and associated content when enforcing community guidelines. Confirmation prompts ("Are you sure?") are required before any deletion action.

4. What Your App Never Saves

  • Financial or Payment Data: Recipe Vault never collects, processes, or stores credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.
  • Device Location Data: Recipe Vault never requests or tracks your GPS coordinates or physical location on desktop or mobile.
  • Third-Party Ad Trackers: Recipe Vault does not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or sell user information to third parties.

Terms of Service

Rules and guidelines for using Recipe Vault.

1. Community Conduct & Content Rules

  • Only upload recipes, images, and PDF documents that are appropriate for a general community audience.
  • Do not upload harmful, abusive, spam, or copyrighted material you do not have permission to share.
  • Be respectful to fellow community members, moderators, and administrators.

2. Account Responsibility

  • You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for all recipes and files posted under your username.
  • Choose an appropriate display name when setting up your profile.

3. Moderation & Enforcement

  • Admins and Moderators reserve the right to send behavior notices, hide posts from public view, remove uploaded files, or delete accounts that violate these terms.
  • Administrators manage staff privileges and may promote or remove moderators at their discretion.

4. Service Availability

  • Recipe Vault is provided "as is" as a community recipe-sharing platform. Always keep personal backups of important recipes or files.
